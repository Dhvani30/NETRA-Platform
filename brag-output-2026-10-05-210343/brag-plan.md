# Brag Plan: NETRA

## What is this app?

NETRA is a social-media intelligence platform that turns public posts, conversations, sentiment, and cross-platform activity into graph-based investigations, alerts, and privacy-safe operational insight.

## The angle

Treat fragmented social activity as an overwhelming field of signals, then show NETRA imposing order: ingest, understand, connect, detect, analyze, respond. The proof is the product's actual intelligence interface—its Deep Search, narrative analytics, live telemetry, alerting, and interactive network graph—not generic cybersecurity decoration.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)

Absolute black. Sparse post fragments, entity labels, timestamps, and location markers materialize until the field is saturated, then arrest in a single frame: **THE SIGNAL IS EVERYWHERE.**

## Key moments (the middle)

- A raw social post is decomposed into entity, topic, sentiment, relationship, and threat-signal layers.
- A controlled relationship graph reorganizes around a suspicious cluster; the graph, not a card grid, is the hero.
- The real command-center design resolves into a Deep Search investigation, live telemetry, alert markers, source modes, and narrative analytics.

## Outro / punchline

The highlighted cluster contracts into NETRA's N mark, then the final statement: **FROM SOCIAL NOISE TO STRATEGIC INTELLIGENCE.**

## User flow worth showing

Enter an intelligence topic in Deep Search → inspect posts, provenance, entity relationships, and a focused subgraph → act on an alert or cross-platform narrative signal in the command center.

## Tone

- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium cyber-intelligence product reveal; precise, credible, and enterprise-grade
- Interpretation: widescreen composition, atmospheric depth, controlled movement, scarce red/orange only for a real threat state, and copy with generous holds.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 60 seconds (explicit user-requested extended launch cut)

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#08091A`
- Accent: `#9A9EE8` with `#7B7FC4` and `#B8BBEE` support
- Text: `#F2F3FA`
- Display font: Space Grotesk
- Body font: Space Grotesk; technical labels in JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: NETRA's dark glass command-center UI and its interactive force-directed network graph.

## Share copy (draft)

NETRA turns fragmented social signals into graph-powered intelligence—so teams can investigate the connections that matter.

## Audio direction

- Role: cinematic support with a restrained electronic pulse
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, treated darker through mix and visual pacing
- Music treatment: slow fade-in from black; 0.32 bed under the film; deepen around the graph reveal; fade under final logo tail
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset at `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; 8.74s NETRA reveal, 17.47s structure lock, 22.93s graph reveal; additional 60-second cues to be detected at composition time
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; cyan/periwinkle atmospheric glow and graph-node presence breathe with RMS, never as waveform graphics
- SFX posture: sparse, premium, motion-matched—soft data clicks, two low impacts, and a final bell
- Audio-coupled moments: fragments entering, entity extraction labels, graph cluster lock, dashboard alert, final N mark
- Restraint rule: no hacker clichés, alarm spam, or high-frequency glitch beds.

## Superseding Creative Direction — Apple × Intelligence Operations

This 60-second cut is not a dashboard demo. It begins with a single white point in total blackness, then a line, then an elegant field of social signal fragments. The visual language is graphite, white, and generous negative space; electric cyan is reserved for intelligence states and subdued red for the one threat state. Data and interface elements behave like precise physical objects in a continuous camera move.

Revised pacing: 0–5s absolute minimalism with `THE WORLD IS TALKING.` then `CAN YOU HEAR THE SIGNAL?`; 5–12s a spatial field of signals resolves from noise to one signal; 12–18s that signal becomes the NETRA symbol and lockup; 18–27s it becomes entities, topic, sentiment, relationship, and a threat state sequentially, with `UNDERSTAND.`, `CONNECT.`, `DETECT.`; 27–37s the graph expands then isolates one meaningful cluster with `FIND THE SIGNAL.` / `HIDDEN IN THE NOISE.`; 37–44s the cluster morphs into a restrained dark India map and one regional pulse with `SEE WHERE IT'S HAPPENING.`; 44–51s the actual NETRA interface grows from map, graph, feed, timeline, and indicator; 51–56s one calm `EMERGING THREAT` alert resolves at `92% CONFIDENCE` with `KNOW WHAT MATTERS.`; 56–60s everything reduces to a point and the final NETRA lockup holds `FROM NOISE TO INTELLIGENCE.`

Do not use generic cyber visuals, hacker language, excessive glow, cluttered cards, rainbow gradients, or abrupt screen changes. The interface must arrive only after the intelligence story earns it.

## Final Story Spine — Chaos into Clarity

The attached brief is now authoritative. The film must play as one continuous transformation, not eight independent scenes: black points (0–5) → information field (5–10) → a first entity connection with `CONNECT` (10–15) → organization into clusters with `UNDERSTAND / THE SIGNAL` (15–22) → a macro view of the NETRA engine turning `RAW DATA → INTELLIGENCE` (22–29) → a huge, isolated anomaly graph with `FIND WHAT MATTERS.` (29–38) → graph-to-India geography (38–44) → only then the minimal constructed NETRA workstation (44–50) → one calm `EMERGING THREAT / 93% CONFIDENCE` state (50–54) → floating interface close-up (54–57) → geometric NETRA reveal and final lockup (57–60).

All visual handoffs are physical continuities: signal → entity → node → graph → cluster → map → interface → mark. The soundtrack begins near silent, gains fine harmonic layers with the graph, drops into a brief silence for anomaly isolation, and ends with a restrained sonic signature. There is no narration.
