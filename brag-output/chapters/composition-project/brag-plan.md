# Brag Plan: NETRA — Chapter 00

## App
NETRA is a social-post intelligence dashboard with sentiment and narrative counts, search, alerts and relationship views.

## Angle and hook
From a stream of posts to an analyst’s first clear view. Open immediately on the recognizable Analytics UI, then guide the viewer from overview to the rest of the product.

## Format and duration
Landscape, 1920x1080, 16:9. 30 seconds. Four visual sections crossfade at 6s, 13s, and 21s; callouts are synchronized to four Kokoro voice segments.

## Visual identity
Background #08091a / #0a0a0f; raised #11122a; accent #9a9ee8 and #b8bbee; text #f2f3fa; display/body Space Grotesk; data labels JetBrains Mono. Recreate the active frontend Analytics tab with safe fictional sample copy and no displayed fabricated counts.

## Voiceover script
1. Posts arrive across social platforms. NETRA brings them together so analysts can see what deserves a closer look.
2. Sentiment Distribution counts stored labels; Emerging Narratives ranks themes by post count.
3. Search a topic, follow narrative change, and compare matching posts across platforms.
4. Then review alerts, audience estimates, and network relationships. Next, see how NETRA produces its numbers.

## Scene plan
- 0–6s — Overview: NETRA Intelligence and faithful Analytics UI. Label as illustrative sample; do not show invented post counts.
- 6–13s — Charts: Sentiment Distribution, Emerging Narratives; callouts “stored labels” and “post count.”
- 13–21s — Workflow: search field, tabs Mutation Tracker and Cross-Platform; callouts match narration.
- 21–30s — Preview chapter cards Alerts, Demographics, Network Intel, Network Graph; close on data-method teaser.

## Audio
Music: vol-12 bundled steady clean track, ducked at 0.13 under VO; fade edges. Cues from bundled 109.96 BPM preset are optional and subordinate to the narrated scene timing. Sparse single soft UI click. Narration: Kokoro af_heart, four segments aligned to scene starts.
