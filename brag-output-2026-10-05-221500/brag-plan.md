# Brag Plan: NETRA

## What is this app?

NETRA is a real-time social intelligence system that turns public cross-platform signals into network, narrative, and threat intelligence while preserving privacy.

## The angle

**A world of signals → a world of intelligence.** This is not a dashboard tour. It is a controlled visual transformation: unstructured public information becomes entities, relationships, geographic context, a detected anomaly, and finally one clear intelligence instrument.

## Hook (0:00–0:07)

Pure obsidian. A lone datum gathers company until a vast, suspended information field surrounds the viewer—then freezes on a single signal and the word **SIGNAL**.

## Key moments

- Fine data particles magnetically resolve into people, organizations, topics, events, locations, and posts.
- An organic relationship graph pulls back into a living map of India, revealing contextual activity density.
- A localized anomaly separates from the field; analytical layers align and confidence rises from 71% to 93%.
- A redesigned analyst instrument demonstrates one continuous investigation: entity → relationships → geography → timeline → evidence → assessment.

## Outro / punchline

The entire intelligence field collapses into a geometric NETRA mark, followed by: **FROM NOISE TO INTELLIGENCE.**

## User flow worth showing

Public signal ingestion → entity/network correlation and geographic context → focused investigation with provenance, timeline, related evidence, and threat assessment.

## Tone

- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium intelligence product reveal; quiet precision, architectural depth, no cyberpunk clichés
- Interpretation: slow, tactile camera motion and editorial typography do the selling. Crimson appears only when the anomaly matters.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 60 seconds

## Visual identity (new visual system)

- Background: `#06080D` obsidian with `#0C1422` midnight atmospheric depth
- Accent: `#66B8FF` electric blue, used as controlled signal light
- Threat: `#E24755` crimson, reserved for detected risk
- Text: `#EDF3FA` soft silver with `#AAB8C8` secondary metadata
- Display font: Georgia / Times New Roman fallback — editorial, restrained
- Body font: Arial / system sans-serif fallback — technical and clean
- Signature: the **Intelligence Field** — particulate data, hairline relationships, and magnetic clusters that persist through every chapter

## Share copy (draft)

NETRA turns public signals into connected threat intelligence—so analysts can see the pattern before it becomes the headline. From noise to intelligence.

## Audio direction

- Role: restrained cinematic support
- Music: original procedural ambient score; near-silent high tones at open, deep pulse as structure forms, a single impact at detection, clean harmonic tail for the mark
- Music treatment: low and spacious; no voiceover; fade beneath the final lockup
- Music cue guidance: custom procedural score; major visual locks at 7s, 32s, 40s, and 57s; timing leads music when readability requires it
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; node glow and atmospheric haze breathe with the low pulse
- SFX posture: sparse and precise—micro signal pings, relationship travel shimmer, one deep threat impact, one restrained logo signature
- Restraint rule: no riser spam, no strobe hits, no “hacker” effects

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Signal field — 7s

One white particle becomes a vast spatial field. Tiny floating metadata includes location, time, topic, entity, sentiment, activity. It resolves to a single luminous datum and **SIGNAL**.

Sequential/interaction: yes — particles arrive in an ordered, accelerating field; metadata drifts on separate depth planes.

Audio intent: nearly silent, with isolated high-frequency ticks and the first low breath.

Transition mood: precise stillness → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Pattern extraction — 8s

A precision scan passes through the datum. Selected atoms lift out into named relationships; lines form and settle magnetically. **PATTERN** dissolves into the connected field.

Sequential/interaction: yes — entity, topic, and location labels separate, then connect.

Audio intent: crystalline scan, then a restrained harmonic lift.

Transition mood: fluid morph → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The living network — 10s

The camera pulls rapidly back through an immense organic graph. Clusters form, fade, and reorganize. A single line of light travels through the network toward a meaningful cluster. **CONNECT EVERYTHING.**

Sequential/interaction: yes — clusters arrive at different depth planes and relationships illuminate in sequence.

Audio intent: wider stereo field and a slow, confident pulse.

Transition mood: camera dive / geographic flattening → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Context — 7s

The graph flattens into a dark, topographic India map. Routes become movement trails; activity density gathers across the subcontinent. One region begins to accelerate. **CONTEXT CHANGES EVERYTHING.**

Sequential/interaction: yes — geographic activity points wake in waves.

Audio intent: pulse gains weight; a distant motion trail follows the camera.

Transition mood: focused convergence → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Detection — 8s

The active region blooms into a localized event. Floating analytical planes label ACTIVITY, SENTIMENT, ENTITIES, RELATIONSHIPS, and ANOMALY. Everything irrelevant falls away; crimson confidence increments 71%, 79%, 86%, 93% and stops.

Sequential/interaction: yes — five analytical layers resolve around a single event; confidence rises in readable steps.

Audio intent: the score narrows, then drops to one deep impact at 93%.

Transition mood: silence and precision → Scene 6.

### Scene 6 — NETRA command environment — 8s

Reveal an original cinematic analyst instrument: an expansive intelligence graph is the dominant surface, with quiet geographic, live-signal, sentiment, and threat layers orbiting it. This is grounded in NETRA’s real graph analytics, timeline, deep search, alerts, and cross-platform correlation capabilities.

Sequential/interaction: none — slow camera-like parallax establishes the space.

Audio intent: the harmonic bed returns, calmer and resolved.

Transition mood: interaction begins → Scene 7.

### Scene 7 — One fluid investigation — 5s

An entity is selected. Its relationships illuminate; the map focuses, the timeline extends, related evidence appears, and the threat assessment updates in one continuous choreography.

Sequential/interaction: yes — select entity → illuminate graph → focus geography → expand timeline → surface evidence → update assessment.

Audio intent: five quiet, material interactions—never UI “click spam.”

Transition mood: elements dematerialize → Scene 8.

### Scene 8 — Hero mark — 4s

The interface disappears. The Intelligence Field remains, rotates, and collapses inward to form the NETRA geometry. Hold.

Sequential/interaction: yes — millions of nodes converge with magnetic momentum.

Audio intent: clean sonic signature at the landing.

Transition mood: cut to black → Scene 9.

### Scene 9 — Final reveal — 3s

NETRA. Network Engine for Threat Recognition & Analytics. **FROM NOISE TO INTELLIGENCE.** Fade to black.

Sequential/interaction: none — immutable final lockup.

Audio intent: fading harmonic tail.
